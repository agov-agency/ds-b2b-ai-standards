# Post builder

Internal page for the launch & distribution kit (Notion "AI Standard launch & distribution kit"): `/post-builder` on any deploy (dev: https://ds-b2b-ai-standards-dev.vercel.app/post-builder). Not linked from any page, `noindex`.

- **Templates** tab: every visual the kit lists, filled from the CMS at build time. Launch and promo (promo visual in 4:5, square, 16:9; site-preview opening and closing frames), newsletter (header 1200×480, "From the Standard" block 1200×240), one card per scenario, skill, rule, quote (Home quotes + rule voices) and author (contributors, most additions first). Publish in Studio and the cards follow after the rebuild.
- **Editor** tab: format (1080, 4:5, 9:16, email 1200×628, header, block, cover 1920×1080), layout (text first / art first / text over art / text only; left-right on wide formats), background (black, white, the 20 cover pastels, any colour), accent, edge lines, dot pattern, logo (full or mark, left or right, size), label, chips, quote mark, two-tone headline (auto-fit or fixed size), body text size (every small text follows it), fade under the text (text on art layout), divider, body, boxes (LinkedIn default vs the Standard, Runs on), steps strip, tools with logos, list, button, author (byline or card, or anyone by name and photo URL), footer text or colour band, art (any scenario figure, hub figure, skill or rule icon, the alternatives, an author photo, or the wave with its own colours), zoom, position, moment, animation.
- **Export**: PNG at 2x; MP4 (H.264, 30 fps, 2x) of one loop of the figure or 8 s of the wave, encoded frame by frame in the browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari 17+). **Copy link** puts the whole post in the URL, so a link reopens it as it was.

Where things live: figures and icons `site/src/art` (pools in `site/src/art/index.ts`, `site/src/icons.ts`), wave `design-system/wave/`, logos `design-system/brand`, connector logos `design-system/logos`, author photos `site/public/authors` or Sanity. Code: `site/src/pages/post-builder.astro` (page, post styles), `site/src/builder.ts` (templates, rendering, export).
